# Enemy audio sources

All recordings are CC0 (commercial use and modification allowed). Existing user-provided effects remain in use.

- Ogrebane — [Monster Sound Effects Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/monster-sound-effects-pack), CC0. Files monster-1 through monster-6.wav become sfx-enemy-growl/undead/beast/death/cast/hurt.mp3, respectively. Short excerpts, loudness normalization to -20 LUFS, fade out, mono MP3.
- crueltyinc — [Little Grunts](https://opengameart.org/content/little-grunts), CC0. sfx-enemy-human.mp3, short excerpt normalized to -21 LUFS.
- VishwaJai — [Dragon Flap](https://opengameart.org/content/dragon-flap-0), CC0. sfx-dragon-wings.mp3, short excerpt, high pass at 70 Hz, -22 LUFS.

Voices follow aggro, attack/cast, taking damage and death; distance falloff up to 55 metres, per-actor throttling, a global voice cap. Passive rabbits and ravens do not use monster growls. Event dragon wings repeat during its flight and respect the sound mute switch.
